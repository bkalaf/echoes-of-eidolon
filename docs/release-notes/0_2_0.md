# Echoes of Eidolon 0.2.0

## Added

### Public world introduction
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /, /features, /gameplay, /about

The new public site introduces Echoes of Eidolon through nine feature stories, captioned media, gameplay and story overviews, a free-to-play commitment, and responsive navigation.

### Secure accounts and beta access
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /auth/sign-in, /auth/sign-up, /account

Players can create and verify accounts, sign in with email or username, manage security settings, request beta invitations, and view account-owned progress, orders, membership, and support records.

### Player support and commerce
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /store, /account/support, /account/orders, /donate

The Store supports privacy-safe guest and account order flows, while account support adds help tickets, order questions, return requests, donations, and optional monthly membership management.

### Governed administration workspaces
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin

Authorized operators can use server-protected workspaces for accounts, invitations, structured data, assets, prompts, commerce, Atlas records, settlements, cities, campaigns, puzzles, capabilities, and operational review.

### Canonical Atlas and settlement operations
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/atlas, /admin/cities, /admin/settlements

Atlas tooling validates the authoritative world dataset, keeps Region and Lattice identities distinct, and supports map, globe, Site, Point of Interest, settlement, population, migration, and city-planning review without fabricating missing world data.

### Release and production safeguards
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/operations, /status

Production operations are pinned to exact source revisions, verify the complete application gate, back up PostgreSQL before migrations, serialize deployments, record redacted outcomes, and retain application rollback support after failed health checks.

## Changed

### Authorization is server-owned
Audience: BOTH
Routes: /account, /admin, /game

Account role, player eligibility, membership benefits, and administrative access are separate server-authorized decisions; client-side visibility never grants access.

### Privacy-first persistence
Audience: BOTH

Sensitive codes and temporary access keys are stored as hashes, provider callbacks are verified and idempotent, public order projections omit private payment details, and ordinary operational logs exclude private conversation content and credentials.

## Fixed

### Stable signed-in navigation
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /

Returning to the home page preserves the active account session and shows the correct signed-in experience.

### Responsive public media
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /, /features

Public navigation remains readable on mobile, the home page uses the approved story artwork, and the captioned feature video fits compact portrait screens while expanding naturally in landscape.

## Security & Privacy

### Hardened account and operations boundaries
Audience: BOTH

Authentication, account ownership, administrative mutations, external bulk access, deployment controls, and destructive world controls fail closed when authorization or required authority is absent.

## Known Issues

### Deferred external and destructive operations
Audience: BOTH

Merchandise fulfillment remains inactive until approved product mappings and provider configuration are installed. Minor participation verification and world reset/reseed operations also remain unavailable until their exact authority is supplied.
