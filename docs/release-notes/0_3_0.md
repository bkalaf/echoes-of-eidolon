# Echoes of Eidolon 0.3.0

## Added

### Owner data live-contract inventory
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/data

Release verification now independently compares the 35 generic Data Registry tables and forms against generated Prisma/Data Registry contracts. Bespoke owner surfaces without an independent owning read or write contract remain blocked instead of being counted as complete. The owner-locked lookup registry renders human-readable labels first, canonical IDs second, searchable identity context, explicit compound labels, and a real null state.

### Companion and occupation authoring
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/campaign/planner, /admin/data/occupations

Administration can author the twelve-companion roster, ordered occupation attributes, world assignments, skills, and Transformation progression through shared domain owners.

### World economy and party status
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /game

The game workspace adds world-specific purse, inventory, withdrawal history, Bank interactions, recovery condition, and Inn meeting surfaces without imposing a fixed inventory capacity.

### Managed settlement soundtracks
Audience: BOTH
Routes: /account/settings, /admin/atlas, /game

Administrators can assign 80 deterministically reconciled city and tavern soundtrack sources to settlements, while players share synchronized mute, master, soundtrack, and narrative volume settings across Account and the game bottom bar.

### Gameplay and World Atlas
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /gameplay, /gameplay/world-atlas

Public Gameplay and World Atlas pages explain the playable experience and provide an explorable public atlas without disclosing player state.

### Initial founding settlement identities
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/atlas

The canonical Atlas import can now verify or transactionally materialize the 24 original physical Settlements and their 72 initial world identities while preserving existing compatible identities and excluding Innerwood until its later world-specific divergence.

### Governed bulk changes
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/bulk-changes, /api/external/bulk

An ordered, typed review queue supports bounded external data changes with dry-run validation, explicit administrative decisions, inactivity expiry, and append-only audit evidence.

### WorldBuilding v3 simple authoring
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/data

Species, Culture, Breed, and Character authoring now uses scalar presentation text, recursive taxonomy, controlled Breed ecology and group classifications, canonical personality expressions, and fail-closed research staging. The retired SpeciesGroup and structured appearance persistence paths are no longer available.

### Witness Puzzle Box production QA and prototype lab
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/puzzles/test-lab, /admin/puzzles/:puzzleBlueprintId/test

The checksum-pinned 70-Blueprint Witness Puzzle Box bank is verify-only by default. Transactional writes require an explicit apply mode and named target environment, and unknown or conflicting command-line flags fail before database access. The four production Blueprints—Ordinal Cancellation Files, Set Union / Intersection Ambigram, Musical Hexadecimal Grid, and Typographic QR Threshold—now mount canonical player renderers inside an owner QA shell. Each has mechanic-specific interaction and submission, two player-facing hints, reset and deterministic regeneration, server-only validation, implemented accessibility equivalents, validation history, and an independently authorized expected-solution reveal that is absent from the player payload. Real Chromium coverage derives and submits each answer through the rendered interaction and rejects an invalid path. The remaining 66 entries are explicitly prototype-only until they independently meet the same standard.

### Member Witness puzzle collection
Audience: BOTH
Routes: /puzzles, /puzzles/quiet-accord, /puzzles/third-reading, /puzzles/the-pall, /puzzles/glass-vespers

Active Members can open four player-safe Witness puzzles from the normal site navigation, while current entitlement is enforced consistently by navigation, server-rendered routes, and no-store APIs. The Quiet Accord now produces a six-character cancellation bitmap across paired signed matrices; The Third Reading presents ambiguous scoped marks as authored cards and a wax seal; The Pall preserves its player-controlled light/dark recovery and ordered symbol passage; and Glass Vespers renders four pages of playable 6/8 SVG sheet music that develops into a high-contrast 32×4 color-glyph field. The same canonical renderers remain available in owner QA without exposing Blueprint identities, versions, seeds, proofs, answers, or intended paths to Members.

## Fixed

### Year-0 public World Atlas
Audience: PLAYERS
Routes: /gameplay/world-atlas

The public globe now projects exactly the 24 canonical original founding cities instead of exposing the general Point of Interest catalog or silently appending a world-specific settlement state. Innerwood remains one of 25 geographically colored Regions but has no Year-0 city marker. The centered desktop globe dominates the available viewport without page scrollbars, while map controls and founding-city detail remain in balanced side rails.

The managed Nimbus terrain is overlaid with authoritative Region boundaries using the requested 12-color, adjacency-safe presentation palette. Players can turn Region tinting off to inspect the underlying terrain, and black Regions retain readable white city-name text. Optional projected labels identify all three continents and 92 simulator-sourced geographic features—including oceans, seas, mountain ranges, peaks, forests, deserts, rivers, lakes, islands, bays, wetlands, and other named physical geography. Labels track globe rotation and disappear when occluded; founding-city controls continue to open city details without stealing globe drag gestures.

### Readable Campaign Planner movement
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/campaign/planner

Campaign cards now expose explicit high-contrast Move up and Move down controls plus a visible drag handle. Reordering preserves each placement identity and Book assignment, persists one same-column position at a time, retains keyboard focus, announces the new position, and keeps cross-column movement separate.

The wide planner board now scrolls inside its own viewport so the Administration shell, title, world tabs, and toolbar stay in place. Record-heavy columns and unassigned pools have readable minimum widths and row heights, while placed cards show human-readable names before canonical IDs.

### Complete application data grids
Audience: BOTH
Routes: /admin, /account, /game, /contact, /tools

The 35 generic Data Registry tables independently preserve their canonical read-model fields and use the shared data-grid contract for typed filters, global search, sorting, sticky identity and action columns, resizing, pagination, counts, empty states, and distinct zebra, hover, focus, and selection states. Bespoke application tables remain release-blocked where their owning read contract or rendered relation-label evidence is unavailable.

### Complete canonical Data Registry forms
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/data

All fields in the 35 generic Data Registry contracts are independently represented in deterministic owner sections: writable fields use typed controls, workflow-owned fields remain visibly read-only, nullable values can be cleared, and relation searches show human-readable labels before canonical IDs. Architect, Witness, and Companion editors include their parent Character context. Bespoke editors remain release-blocked until independent server write contracts and rendered browser evidence are available.

### Deliberate inline Data Registry editing
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/data

Every editable Data Registry row now has an explicit Edit Row action and an accessible checkmark Save action. Only one row enters edit mode, immutable identities remain read-only, typed enum and name-first relation controls are reused in place, and failed validation preserves the administrator's unsaved values. Character subtype rows compose parent Character fields and subtype-owned fields in one atomic canonical save.

### Working Data Registry record routes
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/data/:entityKey/:recordId

Existing Data Registry records now open a generated registry-backed detail route instead of falling through to the application 404. Details are field-complete and read-only first, then expose explicit Edit Record, Save, and Cancel controls through the same canonical PATCH authority used by inline editing, including composed Character subtype changes.

### Full-world 2D Atlas projection
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/atlas/pois, /admin/atlas/sites

Site and Point of Interest workspaces now share one responsive 2:1 EPSG:4326 map stage, keeping the contained world image and marker overlay in the same coordinate space instead of cropping the map beneath unchanged coordinates.

The Site map now supports bounded 1x–6x zoom, wheel zoom, pointer pan, reset, screen-space clustering, and maximum-zoom spiderfy without changing canonical coordinates. Search, map markers, and the Site table share selection; exact classification colors and sizes accompany text labels; classification, Region, occupancy, and Site ID filters stay synchronized. Selecting a World overlays its persisted Settlements with distinct founded markers and prevents Found City on occupied world/Site pairs. The physical-morphology filter remains visibly disabled until the approved `baseMorphology` field is available from the canonical Site read model.

### Import-ready WorldBuilding and companion forms
Audience: OPERATORS
Routes: /admin/data, /admin/campaign/planner, /admin/data/occupations, /admin/atlas

All 34 registered Data entities now resolve every editable field to a typed form control with required/type/list validation and an explicit workflow reason for persisted fields excluded from generic editing. WorldBuilding previews reject canonical identity and presentation drift before apply, canonical names remain paired with immutable persistence IDs, PET-only null fields cannot be authored accidentally, and five-part clothing uses structured controls. Dependency-closed envelopes preserve whole canonical components and persisted references, while authored and inherited presentation require independent static and semantic completion audits. Companion skill participation may remain unassigned, Occupation, Transformation, and settlement soundtrack forms reliably load existing state, and bulk-envelope detail exposes its ordered review actions.

Breed authoring now records the required Human, Beast, Mythos, or Pet population classification independently from biological Species classification, allowing Beast and Pet populations to share one canonical animal Species without leaking sapient presentation into Pet output.

Breed hierarchy now supports one optional canonical parent, with parent-first import ordering and enforced same-Species, same-population, and acyclic relationships for nationality roots and other coverage groupings.

Canonical WorldBuilding packages can now be imported transactionally from explicit, checksum-verified archives. Supplied persistence IDs are preserved exactly, Culture remains an independent root, registry taxonomy may retain authoritative skipped ranks, and intentionally reviewed null personality assignments remain valid.

Species taxonomy is now stored as shared, first-class TAX records with stable identities, canonical parent relationships, readable administration lookups, and reusable reference pages. The production normalization preserves all 1,131 Species references, resolves duplicated legacy node facts deterministically, separates the official and manufactured Draco genera, and removes the superseded embedded JSON only after a lossless relational audit.

Concrete Witness transformation now preserves the source Architect's Soul across two distinct Character records. The 54 department seats use SPONSORSHIP and INNOVATION in place of the retired presiding-role values, while Hans and Noell remain departmentless original Architects outside the ordinary Witness path.

The complete owner-authored Architect and Witness identity set can now be reconciled transactionally and rerun without duplicate rows. Witness definitions use explicit WDF identities, three-color percentage records, and source-Architect Soul links. The Overseer, Herald, and Steward Guide links remain static canonical mappings to existing Characters and Souls rather than database Guide rows. Mother is represented as the explicitly authorized non-biological Character and Soul underlying The Steward; ordinary biological and worldbuilding Characters still require a Breed across supported write paths.

Production promotion now rebuilds the exact server artifact after browser teardown, verifies that artifact before restart, and creates migration backups with owner-only permissions.
