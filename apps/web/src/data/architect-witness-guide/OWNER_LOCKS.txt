- WitnessDef.architectSoulId explicitly identifies the source Architect Soul for that definition.

- Hans, Frank, and Mother each have exactly one underlying Character/Soul used by their Guide representation.
- Guide forms do not create replacement Souls or duplicate Characters.
- Mother is now an underlying Character with SOUL_MOTHER / CHA_MOTHER.
