## Plain-language summary

Eidolon Gaming intends Echoes of Eidolon and its related web experiences to be usable by people with disabilities. This statement describes the accessibility practices the product is working to support, areas that require continuing validation, and how to report barriers. It does not claim full WCAG conformance or independent certification.

## 1. Our accessibility commitment

Eidolon Gaming is committed to improving access to Echoes of Eidolon, account services, legal pages, support flows, and Store experiences. Accessibility is an ongoing engineering and content responsibility rather than a one-time certification. We aim to identify barriers, prioritize issues that prevent meaningful access, and improve features as the product evolves.

## 2. Conformance language

This version does **not** claim that Echoes of Eidolon or every related web page fully conforms to WCAG at any particular level, and it does not represent that an independent audit has certified full conformance. We use recognized accessibility practices and relevant WCAG success criteria as references when designing, testing, and remediating features. Any future conformance claim should be made only after the applicable scope and evidence have been verified.

## 3. Keyboard access and focus

Interactive web controls should be operable without requiring a mouse where keyboard operation is reasonably applicable. Focus order should follow the intended reading and interaction sequence, visible focus states should make the current control understandable, and keyboard users should be able to reach and leave interactive components without becoming trapped.

Complex game interactions can require feature-specific alternatives. If a control cannot yet be completed with a keyboard-equivalent path, that is an accessibility limitation to report rather than something this statement silently treats as solved.

## 4. Motion and animation

Where the interface uses significant motion, camera movement, animated transitions, flashes, or other potentially disruptive effects, we aim to respect reduced-motion preferences where technically appropriate and provide alternatives for essential information that would otherwise be communicated only through motion. Accessibility accommodations should not require a user to give up necessary gameplay information.

## 5. Contrast, text, and visual presentation

We aim for readable contrast, text that can be enlarged without unnecessarily losing information or controls, labels that do not rely solely on color, and layouts that remain understandable at increased zoom or text size where the platform permits. Decorative visual fidelity should not be treated as more important than access to essential instructions, status, or transaction information.

## 6. Audio, captions, and transcripts

Where meaningful prerecorded speech, dialogue, or other audio conveys information needed to understand content, captions, transcripts, or an equivalent text presentation should be provided where applicable and technically feasible. If live or generated audio is introduced, its accessibility support must be evaluated separately rather than assumed to be covered by prerecorded-content practices.

## 7. Puzzles and equivalent presentation

Echoes of Eidolon can include difficult puzzles. Accessibility does not require making every puzzle easy or eliminating the underlying challenge. It does require considering whether a puzzle is unintentionally impossible because essential information is available only through one sensory or motor mode.

Where practical, puzzle information should have an equivalent presentation that preserves the intended reasoning challenge—for example, text equivalents for necessary audio cues, non-color-only indicators, or keyboard-accessible interaction paths. An equivalent presentation may differ in form while preserving the substantive puzzle objective.

## 8. AI-mediated interaction accessibility

AI-mediated characters and systems can produce dynamic text or voice output. Text interaction is not limited by the current Member voice-window perk. We aim to keep essential conversation information available in a form compatible with the interaction mode being used. Dynamic content should not be assumed accessible merely because it is generated as text; focus management, announcements, timing, and interface behavior can also create barriers.

## 10. Third-party services

Some flows rely on third-party services, including Stripe for payment processing and Printful for merchandise fulfillment. Eidolon Gaming should choose and configure integrations with accessibility in mind, but some hosted interfaces may be controlled by the provider. If a third-party barrier prevents a transaction or required action, report it so we can investigate an alternative path or work with the provider where feasible.

## 11. Accessibility feedback

Use `/contact` and select **Accessibility feedback** to report a barrier. Helpful information can include the page or feature, what you were trying to do, the assistive technology or input method involved if you choose to share it, and what happened. Do not include passwords, payment-card data, access tokens, recovery codes, government IDs, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We may acknowledge a report and ask focused follow-up questions. We do not promise a fixed resolution time because severity, technical complexity, provider dependencies, and testing requirements vary. Reports that prevent account access, safety, payment, or core participation should be triaged accordingly.

## 12. Ongoing improvement

Accessibility improvements may be delivered through design changes, code fixes, content corrections, alternative presentations, provider configuration, or documentation. Product updates can also introduce new barriers, so regression testing and user feedback remain important. Material accessibility changes do not remove mandatory rights under applicable law.
