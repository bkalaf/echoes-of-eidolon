## Controlling-document hierarchy

The Terms of Service provide the general contract. When a narrower policy addresses the same transaction more specifically, the narrower policy controls that subject: Membership terms for subscriptions; Donations terms for support contributions; Store Terms for merchandise sale formation; Shipping policy for fulfillment; Returns/Refunds/Cancellation policy for remedies; Beta terms for invitation participation; and the AI, IP, conduct, privacy, cookie, accessibility, and cultural-research policies for their defined subjects. Mandatory law controls over any conflicting contractual limitation.
